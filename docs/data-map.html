    Status life cycles

    The important Status columns and the procedures that change them. The database stores English values; the app translates them through DbValues.

    CLASS.Status

    Enrolling→In progress→Finished
    • usp_Class_Create creates the class as Enrolling; usp_Class_UpdateStatus moves it to In progress.
    • usp_Class_EvaluateResults sets Finished after computing the results; grades and attendance are then locked.
    • Cancelled through usp_Class_UpdateStatus, refused while the class has valid receipts (error 50015); its open enrollments become Left.
    • Finished and Cancelled are final (error 50019): the center opens a new class instead.

    STUDENT.Status

    Prospective→Studying→On holdDropped outCompleted
    • A new student is Prospective; usp_Enrollment_Create moves a Prospective, On hold or Completed student to Studying.
    • usp_Enrollment_UpdateStatus: putting on hold or leaving, with no other Studying enrollment, makes the student On hold or Dropped out; resuming makes the student Studying again.
    • usp_Class_EvaluateResults: a student who takes no other class any more becomes Completed; a new enrollment makes the student Studying again.

    ENROLLMENT.Status

    Studying⇄On hold→LeftCompleted
    • usp_Enrollment_UpdateStatus switches between Studying, On hold and Left.
    • usp_Enrollment_TransferClass keeps the EnrollmentId (receipts and grades stay attached), sets Studying and the tuition of the new class.
    • usp_Class_EvaluateResults writes FinalGrade and Result and sets Completed.

    RECEIPT.Status

    Valid→Cancelled
    • usp_Receipt_Cancel requires a CancelReason; trg_RECEIPT_UpdateAmountPaid lowers ENROLLMENT.AmountPaid again.
    • No DELETE: trg_RECEIPT_PreventDelete blocks it, and even the manager is denied DELETE.

    CLASS_SESSION.Status

    Scheduled→TaughtCancelled
    • usp_Class_GenerateSessions creates Scheduled sessions; usp_Session_Update changes the status and records the content.
    • trg_CLASS_SESSION_LockTaught locks the date, time, room and teacher of a Taught session; usp_Payroll_Finalize pays it.

    ACCOUNT.Status

    Active⇄Locked
    • usp_Account_Lock (manager only) changes Status and denies or grants CONNECT, so a locked account cannot connect.
    • AuthService checks Status again at sign-in.
    i

    What the app has today: sign-in, change password, language switch and a screen with forms for every step of the business flow: catalogs and accounts, students, placement tests, classes and their weekly schedule, enrollments, tuition collection, timetable and attendance, grade book, results, teacher payroll and backup. Every write the app makes goes through a procedure of 04_procedures.sql.

    The read-only reports (Outstanding tuition, Learning results, My classes, My pay) are lists with a quick filter and Excel / PDF export. What a role may change is decided twice: the app hides the buttons (Permissions::canEdit), SQL Server refuses the call (GRANT / DENY).

    Layers of the app

    Dependencies go one way: user interface → application → domain ← infrastructure. src/app (main.cpp, AppContainer) creates the objects of every layer and wires them together.

    Sign-in → role-based menu

    Every user connects to SQL Server with their own account, so what they may read or change is decided by the GRANT / DENY of their role. The app only decides which menu entries to show.
